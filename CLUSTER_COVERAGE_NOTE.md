# Cluster Coverage — Ideas Meaning Candidate

**Lifecycle:** ACTIVE_SUCCESSOR_CANDIDATE  
**Ideas scope:** Meaning / Reader interpretation / Navigation heuristic  
**Runtime / Authority:** false

Architectural scale should not be judged mainly by file count, tool count, window count, or visible complication.

A more useful Ideas-side reading is **coverage fitness**: how much of a heterogeneous field can be understood, related, resumed, and communicated without erasing distinctions or forcing permanent carrier ownership.

## Candidate relation

```text
coverage fitness
≈ relevant breadth
× continuity retention
× differentiation retained
× re-entry ability
× receiver readability
```

This is a heuristic, not a formal metric or Current law.

## What this supersedes

The predecessor tied coverage to one-main-chain runtime, coupling faces, gate/window notes, repository indexing and `return_to_00=true`. Those bindings are superseded.

Coverage does not require:
- fixed windows;
- a central return hub;
- all carriers online at once;
- a permanent router;
- expanding document count.

## Reader rule

Use this concept when comparing architectural breadth or discussing whether a system can span heterogeneous domains without losing identity and continuity. Do not use it to infer Runtime, Authority, maturity, or superiority.

Material operational metrics remain evidence-dependent and may be owned elsewhere when they affect state, capability, cost, performance, or world admission.
